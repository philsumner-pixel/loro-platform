Reporting lag. The Electoral Commission publishes on a delay — parties report quarterly, regulated donees monthly — so recent months are always incomplete. Every window here ends at the last fully reported month rather than today, so periods stay comparable. Party colours are used for identification; party logos are trademarks and are not reproduced.
