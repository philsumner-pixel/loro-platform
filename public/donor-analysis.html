How concentrated is each party's funding?

Each bar is the share of a party's money coming from its single largest donor, its top three, and its top ten. A long, bright first segment means a party depends on very few people — and it pulses harder the more concentrated it is.

Who gives to more than one recipient

Donors on the left, recipients on the right — each thread is a live current of money. Only those giving to two or more appear: the people and companies funding across party lines, which is a different question from who funds whom. Hover a name to trace it.

When the money arrives

Reported donations by week. Clustering ahead of an election, a leadership contest or a significant vote is the pattern worth asking about. Hover a week for the split.

Reporting lag. Windows end at the last fully reported month — the Electoral Commission publishes on a delay, so recent months are always incomplete. Donations are lawful and shown as public record. Donor identities are consolidated across name variants by a reviewed matching process; where a match was uncertain the records are left separate.
